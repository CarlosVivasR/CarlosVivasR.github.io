What decides whether a third-placed team advances?

Tap to see the FIFA tiebreakers and how the bracket fills in.

The 2026 World Cup has 12 groups of 4 teams. The top 2 from each group qualify directly for the Round of 32 (24 teams). To complete the 32-team knockout bracket, FIFA ranks all 12 third-placed teams in a single table and keeps the top 8; the worst 4 are eliminated.

Tiebreakers applied in this exact order (as soon as one separates two teams, it decides):

  1. Points earned in the group stage
  2. Goal difference
  3. Goals scored
  4. Fair play (yellow / red cards)
  5. FIFA ranking before the tournament

That's why here you mark with ★ the 8 third-placed teams you think will end up with the best balance of points and goals. The bracket is then populated following the FIFA rule: each third-place slot can only receive teams from 5 specific groups (Annex C of the regulations), and never a team from the same group as its opponent.
