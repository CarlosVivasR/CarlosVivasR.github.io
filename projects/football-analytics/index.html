1. The Hidden Value Map

What is Hidden Value?

Creative Score v2 combines 11 metrics per 90 min (key passes, big chances created, xG, dribbles, shots, assists, npG, fouls drawn, crosses, tackles, interceptions), each weighted and percentile-ranked vs all La Liga MF/FW.
Visibility = goals + assists percentile (what everyone sees).
Hidden Value = Creative Score − Visibility. A positive gap means the player contributes more than their G+A numbers suggest.

Methodology

Creative Score v2 is a weighted composite of per-90 metrics, each percentile-ranked against all La Liga MF/FW with 8+ full 90s played (n=194):

MetricWeight
KeyPasses/901.5
BigChancesCreated/901.5
Assists/901.0
Non-PK Goals/901.0
xG/900.8
SuccessfulDribbles/900.8
Shots/900.6
FoulsDrawn/900.6
Crosses/900.4
TacklesWon/900.3
Interceptions/900.3

Each metric is converted to a percentile (0-100) within the cohort, multiplied by its weight, then all weighted percentiles are summed and rescaled to a 0-100 index.

Visibility is the G+A per 90 percentile rank.
Hidden Value v2 = Creative Score v2 − Visibility. A positive gap means the player contributes more than their goal/assist numbers suggest.

Data sources: FBref.com (powered by StatsBomb) and Sofascore. All stats from the 2025-26 La Liga season as of April 2026.
