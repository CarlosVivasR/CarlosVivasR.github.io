Lamine Yamal: individual report

Spain · UEFA Euro 2024 · 7 matches, 531 minutes · Data: StatsBomb Open Data

A one-page player report of the kind I would hand to an academy coach: where the player gets on the ball, what he does with it, how he defends, and how that compares with players in the same role at the same tournament.

Why this player

He was the youngest player at the tournament and an academy graduate playing a starting role against senior internationals, which is the question an academy department cares about: what does a young player already do at the top level, and what still needs work? The report uses all 7 of Spain's matches rather than one game, because one match is too small a sample for a wide forward who may only get a handful of shots.

Strengths

  1. Chance creation. 0.36 xG assisted per 90, ranked 1 of 21 peers (percentile 98), from 18 shot assists and 4 assists. 72% of those shot assists started in the right channel and 100% were left-footed.
  2. Carrying the ball forward. 6.61 progressive carries per 90 (percentile 83). 86% of his touches were in the opposition half and 84% in the right channel: he is a reliable outlet who takes the ball up the pitch himself.
  3. Work without the ball. 19.0 pressures per 90 (percentile 93), 63% of them in the opposition half, and percentile 88 for tackles plus interceptions won.

Areas to improve

  1. Ball security. 4.07 turnovers per 90 against a peer median of 3.00 (percentile 17, where higher means fewer). He completed 12 of 27 dribbles (44%, peer median 57%), and 7 of his 24 turnovers were in his own half. Coaching point: when to take the defender on and when to recycle.
  2. A second option. All 18 of his shots and all 4 assists were left-footed, and 91% of his foot passes. Shot selection is not the issue (0.088 xG per shot against a peer median of 0.068), and 1 goal from 1.59 non-penalty xG over 18 shots is well within normal variation. The development point is being harder to read: a right-foot finish or an outside run to the byline.

Method and caveats

  • Sample size. 531 minutes and 18 shots. Rates like xG per 90 are noisy at this volume, so the strengths above rest on several metrics pointing the same way, not on any single number.
  • Team context. Spain dominated the ball, which inflates attacking volume and lowers defensive volume for their players. Per-90 numbers are not adjusted for possession.
  • Definitions. xG is StatsBomb's model; penalties are excluded from xG and shots. Minutes come from lineup substitution times. Turnovers are dispossessions plus miscontrols. Peer position is the one a player spent most minutes in.
